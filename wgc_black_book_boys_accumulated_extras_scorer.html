Black Book Accumulated Extras Rules:

  • Today's Total: Starting Extra + Score for the Day (e.g. +2 + 26 = 28).
  • 18th Hole Rule: Answering YES adds +1 to the carried-forward balance.
  • Outright Winner's Cut: 1 + (Total − 36, if >36) + (Margin over 2nd) subtracted from carried-forward balance.
  • Tie Rule: If 2 or more tie for 1st, there is no winner, no cut for tied players, and all £2 entries are returned.
